# Block Party Burgers: test builds

Downloads of Block Party Burgers for playing together. Just the game, no code.

## Get the game

1. Open the **[newest build](https://github.com/citrus-lemonjelly/block-party-builds/releases/latest)**
   and download `BlockPartyBurgers-windows.zip` under **Assets**, or download it straight away:
   [BlockPartyBurgers-windows.zip](https://github.com/citrus-lemonjelly/block-party-builds/releases/latest/download/BlockPartyBurgers-windows.zip)
   (always the newest build).
2. Unzip the whole folder (replace any old copy). Keep the two `.dll` files next to
   `BlockPartyBurgers.exe`.
3. Open Steam first, then the game.

Windows only for now. Windows may warn about an unknown app the first time: choose
**More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Playing together

- **Host:** on the in-game phone, **Host**, then **Open the cart**. Invite friends from the game:
  press **Tab** for your phone, then **Friends**.
- **Join:** on the phone, **Join**, and pick your friend under "Friends with their cart open",
  or accept their Steam invite with the game open. You need to be Steam friends.
- Up to 4 players. Everyone needs the **same build**: the main menu shows it in the bottom-left
  corner (for example "Build 0.0.1 60df29"). If yours differ, joining tells you; download the
  newest build.

Steam shows the game as "Spacewar" (Steam's test app) until it has its own.
